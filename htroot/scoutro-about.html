About Scoutro

Scoutro is a mobile-first search, crawling and indexing application. Scoutro is an independent community project based on YaCy, the free, distributed peer-to-peer search engine.

YaCy is developed by Michael Peter Christen and the YaCy contributors (the YaCy project). Scoutro is not affiliated with or endorsed by the YaCy project.
