Correction proposals

The correction automation checks whether a website is in the wrong specialist collection, from independent signals only (the website's structured data and text, the Discovery classification, OpenStreetMap). It is off unless switched on in the settings; it changes a collection automatically only after a statistical acceptance of a random sample of reviewed proposals. Approving a proposal is a manual collection decision.
