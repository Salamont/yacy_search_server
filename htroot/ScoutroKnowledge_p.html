Storage

JSON-LD in the Solr index

Levels: from 70 % a notice, from 80 % a warning, from 90 % new growth pauses (JSON-LD: the capture pauses, pages are indexed without it), the budget is the hard limit. Crawling and indexing never stop because of these budgets.

Synchronisation

LLM tier

Optional. Statements only the LLM tier supports are shown as uncertain; every one carries a quote from the page. The model is chosen in LLM Selection (usage knowledge).

Vocabulary and derived relations

Industries follow NACE Rev. 2.1 / WZ 2025; service categories and audiences come from the Scoutro vocabularies of each collection. Derived relations (same operator, weak link signals, suggested customers and partners) are no facts; they are recomputed every interval and only shown to a viewer of both collections.

Collections

With scoutro.kg.collections=* a new collection is followed at once; with a fixed list only the named ones. A collection without a vocabulary gets generic facts only (names, addresses, contacts, relations), no service categories; its vocabulary is never guessed from its name. Assign an existing one with scoutro.kg.vocab.<collection>=<vocabulary> or a new one with a file in DATA/SCOUTRO/knowledge/vocabulary. Jobs are read only for the collections named in scoutro.kg.jobs.collections.

Controls

Pausing stops new enrichment (extraction, the LLM tier and a rebuild); deletions, reconcile and state checks continue. Nothing here changes the Solr index.

Backups

Backups are single SQLite files in DATA/SCOUTRO/knowledge/backup, inside the app's data; download one to keep a copy elsewhere. A restore replaces the graph with the backup, keeps the current graph aside as a backup, starts a new dataset epoch (export consumers sync again) and reconciles with the index.

Re-resolve identities

Builds the whole graph anew from the Solr index with the current identity rules, in DATA/SCOUTRO/knowledge/rebuild and within the remaining storage budget, while the current graph keeps serving: wrong merges are split, every fact keeps its evidence. Before the swap the rebuilt graph is checked; if it would lose many documents, it waits for your confirmation like a reconcile. The current graph is kept as a backup (before a rebuild), stored entity IDs keep leading to their objects, and export consumers sync again. Cancelling, or stopping Scoutro, before the swap deletes the rebuilt graph and changes nothing.

Network

All providers of one service name, never the whole graph. The centre is the name only, no object: every line is one provider's own offer of its own service, with that service's own prices, sources and collections. Services, prices and facts of different providers are never merged or mixed. Tap a provider or a line for its own service; the list shows each line with that service.

The neighbourhood of one object, never the whole graph. Arrows point in the direction the source states: a provider offers a service, a company is a partner of another. Solid lines are facts with evidence, dashed lines uncertain facts, dotted lines derived rows, which are no facts: a shared operator, a weak link signal or a suggestion. Tap a node or a line for its details, its relation to the centre and its sources; around a service its providers are shown, around a job its employer.

    Settings

    Settings are stored in Scoutro's configuration (keys scoutro.kg.*, for example in Advanced Properties) and take effect at the next start. Invalid values are listed here and keep the graph off.

    Collections in the knowledge graph

    Every collection of the catalog is listed here, a new one too; YaCy's internal collections never. Switching a collection off keeps its graph data: nothing is deleted and nothing new is read from it. Switching it on reads the pages it already has in the index. A change concerns only this collection and takes effect at once (the graph is reopened). A new vocabulary of a followed collection makes the graph read its pages again in the background. Settings keys:

    scoutro.kg.collections, scoutro.kg.collections.inactive, scoutro.kg.vocab.<collection>
