Technical diagnosis

State, storage, queues and events describe the whole graph; the number of objects follows the selected collection.

Storage

JSON-LD in the Solr index

Levels: from 70 % a notice, from 80 % a warning, from 90 % new growth pauses (JSON-LD: the capture pauses, pages are indexed without it), the budget is the hard limit. Crawling and indexing never stop because of these budgets.

Synchronisation

LLM tier

Calls and items count since the last start of Scoutro; every restart resets them.

Invalid items by reason (since the last start)

Single entities, claims and values the validator dropped, each under the first rule it breaks; not failed calls and not refused answers. Diagnostic counters only; no model answer is stored.

Optional. Statements only the LLM tier supports are shown as uncertain; every one carries a quote from the page. The model is chosen in LLM Selection (usage knowledge).

Vocabulary and derived relations

Industries follow NACE Rev. 2.1 / WZ 2025; service categories and audiences come from the Scoutro vocabularies of each collection. Derived relations (same operator, weak link signals, suggested customers and partners) are no facts; they are recomputed every interval and only shown to a viewer of both collections.

With scoutro.kg.collections=* a new collection is followed at once; with a fixed list only the named ones. A collection without a vocabulary gets generic facts only (names, addresses, contacts, relations), no service categories; its vocabulary is never guessed from its name. Assign an existing one with scoutro.kg.vocab.<collection>=<vocabulary> or a new one with a file in DATA/SCOUTRO/knowledge/vocabulary. Job objects are read only for the collections named in scoutro.kg.jobs.collections or switched on in the settings; the software hints of job advertisements are read for every collection unless switched off (scoutro.kg.jobs.signals).

Controls

Pausing stops new enrichment (extraction, the LLM tier and a rebuild); deletions, reconcile and state checks continue. Nothing here changes the Solr index.

Backups

Backups are single SQLite files in DATA/SCOUTRO/knowledge/backup, inside the app's data; download one to keep a copy elsewhere. A restore replaces the graph with the backup, keeps the current graph aside as a backup, starts a new dataset epoch (export consumers sync again) and reconciles with the index.

Re-resolve identities

Builds the whole graph anew from the Solr index with the current identity rules, in DATA/SCOUTRO/knowledge/rebuild and within the remaining storage budget, while the current graph keeps serving: wrong merges are split. Durable observations, corrections and current access classifications are carried at the final swap; live facts are reconstructed from the current index. Before the swap the rebuilt graph is checked; if it would lose many documents, it waits for your confirmation like a reconcile. The current graph is kept as a backup (before a rebuild), stored entity IDs keep leading to their objects, and export consumers sync again. Cancelling, or stopping Scoutro, before the swap deletes the rebuilt graph and changes nothing.

    Settings

    General settings take effect at the next start; collection settings and LLM timing apply immediately. Invalid general settings keep the graph off; an invalid timing plan pauses only LLM calls.

    Timing of LLM enrichment

    Allowed starts, not guaranteed completion times. Overnight windows belong to their starting weekday; equal times mean 24 hours. Clock-change gaps have no starts; both repeated times are allowed. Manual runs last at most one hour and keep all safety checks. Completed pages are not reevaluated; failed pages need Retry.

    Weekdays (window starts on these days)

    Collections in the knowledge graph

    Every collection of the catalog is listed here, a new one too; YaCy's internal collections never. Switching a collection off keeps its graph data: nothing is deleted and nothing new is read from it. Switching it on reads the pages it already has in the index. A change concerns only this collection and takes effect at once (the graph is reopened). A new vocabulary of a followed collection makes the graph read its pages again in the background. Settings keys:

    scoutro.kg.collections, scoutro.kg.collections.inactive, scoutro.kg.vocab.<collection>, scoutro.kg.llm.collections

    The LLM enrichment reads the pages of the collections switched on for it in addition, with the model of the LLM selection that has the usage knowledge; there is no second model setting. It is never switched on by itself, and without it the knowledge graph works as before.

    LLM selection

    The four job policies are independent and none of them is a permission; switching one off deletes no knowledge.

    • Extract jobs as objects: job objects with title, employer, place, salary and dates. Changing it reads every page of every collection again.
    • Read software hints from job ads (on unless switched off): software and system mentions of a job advertisement are kept for its declared employer, also without job objects. A required or desirable skill stays a requirement, never a proof of own use or of a purchase; "we use" counts as internal use, work for clients as client work; an advertisement of an intermediary is attributed to no company. Changing it checks the pages of the collection again and reads only its job pages again.
    • Show job objects: whether job objects appear in the company view and in the chat. It hides nothing else and changes no extraction; software hints stay visible at the company.
    • Use stored job signals for matching: whether hints from job objects and job advertisements may feed customer suggestions; a requirement alone stays a weak signal.

    Assessments

    An assessment corrects what one source, host or domain supports: it withdraws its evidence for a predicate, moves the evidence to another predicate, or adds an operator statement with the source's own words. The same fact from an independent source stays. Every assessment holds across new readings, the AI cache and recrawls, is shown here with its effect and is undone with Revoke. Nothing is sent to a model and nothing in the index changes.

    Withdraw: value, subject and tier narrow it down (without them every value of the predicate in the scope). Move: needs a target predicate with the same kind of value. Operator statement: one source, a subject, a value and a quote that is in the source's text.
